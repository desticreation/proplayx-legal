2. How we share data

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as needed to run the Services:

  • Venue and coaching partners – your name and mobile number for the booking you make with them.
  • Cashfree Payments – to process your payment.
  • SMS / OTP provider – your mobile number, to send login OTPs.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging – your push-notification token, to deliver notifications.
  • Hosting and IT providers – who store our data securely on our behalf.
  • Authorities – where required by law or to protect our users and Services.

We require every third party we share personal data with to protect it with the same or equal level of protection as described in this policy and as required by applicable law, and to use it only for the purpose we shared it for.

3. How long we keep data

We keep your personal data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except transaction records that we must keep for tax and accounting purposes under Indian law, which are kept only for the period the law requires.

4. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: tap the profile icon (top right), open the Settings tab, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account, then confirm.
  • By email: write to contact@proplayx.in from your registered email, or mention your registered mobile number.

5. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate another person to exercise your rights. You can turn off push notifications at any time in your device settings. To make a request, email contact@proplayx.in.

6. Children

The Services are meant for users aged 18 and above. A parent or guardian may make bookings or coaching enrolments on behalf of a minor. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children without parental consent.

7. Security

All data between the app and our servers is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to personal data is limited to staff and partners who need it to provide the Services.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date, and notify you in the app for significant changes.

9. Contact and grievances

For any privacy question, request or grievance, contact the ProPlayX team. We aim to respond within 7 working days.
